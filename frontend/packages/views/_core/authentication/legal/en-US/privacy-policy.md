## 2. Information We Collect

We follow the principles of lawfulness, fairness, necessity and good faith, and collect only the information necessary to provide the features of the Service.

### 1. Information You Provide to Us

- **Registration information**: your username, email address and password. Passwords are encrypted in transit and stored only as irreversible hashes, so we cannot see your password in plaintext.
- **Profile information**: your nickname, avatar, mobile phone number and other details you choose to add on your Profile page.
- **Authentication information**: the verification information required when you enable two-factor authentication or sign in with a verification code sent to your mobile phone or email.
- **Third-party account information**: if you choose to sign in with a third-party account, the account identifier, nickname, avatar and email address we obtain from that third party with your authorization.

### 2. Information Collected Automatically When You Use the Service

- **Log information**: sign-in times and results, IP addresses, the APIs accessed and operations performed, and error information, which we use for security auditing and troubleshooting and to meet log retention requirements under applicable laws and regulations.
- **Device and browser information**: browser type and version, operating system, and language and time zone settings, which we use to adapt the display and to detect unusual sign-in activity.
- **Session information**: sign-in tokens and session identifiers, which we use to keep you signed in.

### 3. Circumstances Where Consent Is Not Required

In accordance with applicable laws and regulations, we may process your personal information without obtaining your separate consent in certain circumstances, such as where the processing is necessary to enter into or perform a contract to which you are a party, to fulfill our statutory duties or legal obligations, or to respond to an emergency or protect your life, health or property.
